# Business Blueprint: AI Automation + Digital Products (Bangalore, Nov 2026 → Jan 2027 and beyond)

Owner: Gaurav. Execution partner: Claude. Start: Mon 2 Nov 2026. First review gate: Sun 29 Nov. 12-week mark: Sun 24 Jan 2027.

> Honesty first. ₹2–3L/month inside 12 weeks is a stretch target, not a forecast. Sources I found (mostly vendor blogs, unverified) suggest beginners reach ₹20–40k/month by month 3 and agency founders take 6–12 months to reach $2–10k/month. This plan is built to maximise the odds, with explicit pivot gates so you never spend 3 months on something that isn't working.

## 2. Strategy: one engine, two assets

| Layer | What | Why | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Engine (income now)** | Productised AI/n8n automation for Indian SMBs, then global clients | Services are the only category with a plausible path to ₹1L+/month in months, not years | 70% of your hours |
| **Asset 1 (compounding)** | Digital products: workflow packs, Sheets/Notion templates, profession-specific prompt packs | Built from the same work as client delivery, so near-zero extra effort; sells while you sleep | 20% |
| **Asset 2 (long game)** | YouTube, "build in public" automation videos | Free lead engine and credibility. Direct YouTube income is slow (RPM roughly ₹50–200 per 1,000 views per sources) | 10% |

Everything feeds the others: each client build becomes a template product and a video.

## 3. The offers (price ladder)

Prices are starting hypotheses. Raise them after 3 closed deals.

1. **Lead magnet (free):** "7 automations every {niche} business should run": PDF + 3-minute demo video. Collects emails/DMs.
2. **Low ticket, ₹299–1,999 (Gumroad / Lemon Squeezy):** n8n starter packs, GST/invoice trackers, prompt packs per profession.
3. **Core service, ₹15–40k setup + ₹5–15k/month retainer:** "Missed-Lead Rescue System" (flagship, see below).
4. **Premium, ₹60k–2L project:** custom multi-system automations, AI agents, dashboards.
5. **Global (USD) via Upwork/LinkedIn:** same workflows, 3–5x the price. Start after you have 1–2 case studies.

### Flagship: "Missed-Lead Rescue System"
For businesses that get enquiries through forms, Instagram, WhatsApp, calls and lose them:
- Enquiry arrives (web form / WhatsApp / Meta lead ad) → logged in a sheet/CRM → instant acknowledgement message → follow-up sequence over 3–7 days → owner gets a daily "who to call" digest → missed leads flagged.
- Why it sells: the pain is measurable in rupees (leads lost), the build is 1–2 days, the maintenance retainer is natural.

### Target niches, ranked (my judgment; validate in week 1)
1. Clinics, dental, skin, physio. High lead value, always missing calls.
2. Real estate brokers and small builders.
3. Coaching and tuition institutes, study-abroad consultants.
4. D2C and Instagram sellers (order updates, review requests).
5. CA/accounting firms (document and payment reminders).

## 4. Tools and platforms

- **Automation stack:** n8n (self-hosted via Docker on a small VPS, or n8n Cloud trial first), Google Sheets/Airtable as CRM, WhatsApp via an official provider (check current pricing of Interakt/WATI/Gupshup/Meta Cloud API yourself; I could not verify fees). Zapier/Gumloop/Make only when a client already uses them.
- **Selling products:** Gumroad (10% + $0.50 direct, Merchant of Record, $100 minimum payout, per sources), Lemon Squeezy (about 5% + $0.50, slower settlement, per sources). Etsy is only worth it for printable/Canva-style items; for automation products Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy plus your own site fits better. Check n8n's template/creator programme for distribution (verify current rules).
- **Selling services:** direct outreach (LinkedIn, email, warm intros), Upwork, Contra, Topmate (10% fee, good for paid 1:1 calls), local WhatsApp/LinkedIn communities.
- **Payments for services:** UPI/bank transfer or Razorpay invoicing; USD via Wise/Upwork.
- **Ads:** none until something converts organically. Then Meta ads for products (₹300–500/day test) and Meta lead ads as a case study for clients.

## 5. Compliance checklist (verify with a CA/lawyer; I'm not one)

- **Employment contract:** check moonlighting, conflict-of-interest and IP clauses before taking any paid client. Highest priority, task 1.
- **WhatsApp:** only message people who opted in. Cold bulk WhatsApp gets numbers banned and may break platform rules.
- **Cold outreach:** personalise, keep volume human, offer opt-out. No scraping that violates a site's terms.
- **Tax:** freelance income is taxable. GST registration thresholds and export-of-services rules apply; get a CA for 1 hour.
- **Client data:** never store client customer data in a public repo or shared sheet.

## 6. Roadmap (12 weeks)

**Time assumption:** ~25 hours/week (about 2–3 hours on weekdays after your shift, 8–10 on weekends). Tell me if that's wrong.

### Phase 0: Foundations (Week 1, 2–8 Nov)
Contract check, accounts, n8n running, 10 customer-discovery chats, brand and positioning.
**Exit:** n8n live, 10 conversations logged, niche #1 chosen from real replies.

### Phase 1: Build and start selling (Weeks 2–4, 9–29 Nov)
Build 3 demo workflows, landing page, outreach scripts, prospect list of 100. Start outreach (20 personalised messages per weekday). List first 3 products. Record YouTube video #1. Offer 2 discounted pilots for testimonials.
**Gate 1 (29 Nov):** ≥100 outreach sent, ≥10 discovery calls, ≥2 pilots started. If under 5 calls, change the message, niche or channel before continuing.

### Phase 2: Close and deliver (Weeks 5–8, 30 Nov–27 Dec)
Convert pilots to paid, deliver, collect case studies, ask for referrals, Upwork profile and proposals, products 4–6, 4 YouTube videos, first small ad test on a product.
**Gate 2 (27 Dec):** ≥1 paying client (₹10k+ collected), ≥1 case study. If zero paid by here, stop and diagnose offer, price or audience with me before spending more.

### Phase 3: Systemise and scale (Weeks 9–12, 28 Dec–24 Jan)
Raise prices, retainers, referral ask, agency partnerships (web/marketing agencies who need automation but don't build it), productise the best-selling workflow, content repurposing.
**Gate 3 (24 Jan):** decide: double down, pivot niche, or add the next layer.

## 8. Weekly operating system

- **Mon (30 min):** Claude and you review last week's numbers, set this week's top 3.
- **Daily:** 20 outreach, 1 follow-up block, 1 build/content block.
- **Fri:** log KPIs in Notion, I update the plan.
- **Kill rules:** if a channel produces zero replies in 100 messages, change the message; if a niche gives zero calls in 50 messages, change the niche.

### KPIs to track every week
Outreach sent · replies · calls booked · proposals sent · deals closed · ₹ collected · product sales · YouTube videos posted · subscribers.

## 9. YouTube (long game, no pressure)

- Format: weekly 5–10 minute "I automate X for a {niche} business" screen-recording builds, plus 2–3 Shorts cut from each.
- Goal for 12 weeks is consistency (12 videos), not monetisation. YPP needs 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours (or 10M Shorts views in 90 days) per sources.
- Every video ends with a link to the lead magnet or product.
- I write scripts, titles, thumbnails text and descriptions; you record.

## 10. Risks

- Slow first client (most likely). Mitigation: pilots, warm intros, price-in-rupees pitch.
- Burnout from job plus this. Mitigation: 25 hours/week cap, one full day off.
- Platform/account bans. Mitigation: official APIs only, opt-in messaging.
- "Agency wall" at about ₹5k/month for solo builders per sources. Mitigation: productise, templates, retainers, later hire a part-time helper.
- Over-building before selling. Rule: no new build without a person who said they'd pay.
